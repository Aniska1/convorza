Opening hours: Open every day, 11am to 11pm.
Reservations: Tables can be booked for 1 to 9 people. Parties of 10 or more need manager approval.
Menu: North Indian, tandoor and biryani. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian options. Butter Chicken 380 rupees, Paneer Tikka 320 rupees, Veg Biryani 280 rupees, Chicken Biryani 340 rupees.
Allergies: Our kitchen uses nuts and dairy. For allergy questions the manager must confirm safe options.
Delivery: We deliver within 5 km. Delivery time is 40 to 50 minutes. Minimum order is 300 rupees.
Cancellations: Reservations can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the booking time.
